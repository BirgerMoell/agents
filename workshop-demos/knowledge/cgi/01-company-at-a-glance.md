# CGI at a glance

Source: https://www.cgi.com/en/about-us/cgi-at-a-glance
Retrieved: 2026-09-27

## Scale and business

CGI was founded in 1976 and is one of the world's largest IT and business consulting
services firms. For fiscal 2025, CGI reported C$15.9 billion in revenue, 94,000 consultants
and professionals, 5,500 end-to-end services clients, and 400 locations worldwide. CGI is
listed on the New York Stock Exchange as GIB and on the Toronto Stock Exchange as GIB.A.

## Client proximity

CGI describes its operating model as combining local proximity with global scale. Teams
embed operations in clients' metropolitan markets to build long-term relationships and
understand local business and mission needs. Those teams can draw on CGI's wider global
network for talent, innovation, delivery capacity, and industry expertise.

## Industry approach

CGI emphasizes practical solutions grounded in both technology and industry knowledge.
Its consultants map a client's operating architecture to CGI's offering roadmap so that
services and solutions can be adapted to the client's context rather than treated as a
generic technology deployment.
