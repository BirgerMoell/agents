# CGI approach to generative AI

Source: https://www.cgi.com/en/artificial-intelligence/generative-ai
Retrieved: 2026-09-27

## From experiments to business outcomes

CGI treats generative AI as part of a broader solution rather than as a standalone
technology. It starts with business or government mission problems and may combine GenAI
with digital twins, conversational AI, traditional AI, responsible data practices, and
cloud foundations. CGI says this combination should make solutions scalable and connect
them to demonstrable return on investment.

## Adoption and delivery

CGI's Voice of Our Clients research reports that 80% of executives say their organizations
are exploring generative AI. CGI describes examples such as regulatory and financial
planning support, M&A strategy, operational efficiency planning, client relationship
management, and strategic business planning. CGI also experiments internally with use
cases including bid-response generation and employee-sentiment analysis.

## Trusted implementation

CGI says its GenAI delivery combines scientific rigor, human-centered principles, and
industry expertise. Its CGI PulseAI offering is intended to accelerate use cases by
abstracting underlying technologies, embedding responsible-use principles, and
integrating AIOps. CGI applies Explainable AI techniques and considers emerging regulation
across the delivery lifecycle, from strategy through operations.
