# CGI history and identity

Source: https://www.cgi.com/en/about-us/cgi-history
Retrieved: 2026-09-27

## Founding

Serge Godin and André Imbeau founded CGI in Québec City, Canada, in June 1976. The name
CGI comes from the French "Conseillers en gestion et informatique," meaning information
systems and management consultants. In English, CGI has also been interpreted as
"Consultants to Government and Industry."

## Ownership culture

The founders described their shared dream as creating an environment in which people
enjoy working together and, as owners, contribute to building a company they can be proud
of. CGI refers to its employees as CGI Partners, reflecting this ownership culture.

## Growth and AI investment

CGI expanded through organic growth and combinations, including the 2012 agreement with
Logica, which increased its workforce to 68,000 and strengthened its reach across the
Americas, Europe, and Asia Pacific. In 2023, CGI announced a plan to invest C$1 billion in
expanding AI capabilities to help clients create responsible strategies focused on return
on investment. At the end of fiscal 2025, CGI reported 94,000 CGI Partners and C$15.9
billion in annual revenue.
