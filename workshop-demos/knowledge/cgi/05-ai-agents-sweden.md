# AI agents at CGI Sweden

Source: https://www.cgi.com/se/sv/artificial-intelligence/ai-agenter
Retrieved: 2026-09-27

## What AI agents are

CGI Sweden describes AI agents as intelligent systems that can perceive, make decisions,
and act. More advanced agents can learn, adapt, and produce dynamic results. CGI presents
human-and-AI collaboration as a way to increase quality and speed by orchestrating work,
automating routine tasks, and providing dynamic support.

## Example use cases

The page lists agent use cases including analyzing financial data and producing insights,
matching consultants to roles and updating CVs, producing supply-chain recommendations,
and automating customer-support triage and responses.

## Human-agent governance

CGI's framework for collaboration between humans and AI agents calls for clear roles and
responsibilities, lifecycle oversight from deployment through retirement, ethical
governance and transparent decisions, performance monitoring and optimization, and
protocols for smooth collaboration. CGI highlights on-premises and cloud-ready solutions
as options for control and flexibility.

## Adoption path

CGI Sweden organizes adoption into four stages: Envision to create a strategy, Explore to
identify use cases, Engineer to build the ecosystem, and Expand to scale sustainably. It
mentions CGI PulseAI, Orchestrated AI, and DeepContext as modular accelerators intended to
help organizations move faster toward enterprise-ready implementations.
